The Art of CSS Typography

By John Doe January 15, 2024

Typography is one of the most important aspects of web design. It affects readability, user experience, and the overall aesthetic of your website.

Why Typography Matters

Good typography makes content easy to read and understand. It creates hierarchy, guides the reader's eye, and establishes the tone of your content.

Key Principles

  • Choose readable fonts
  • Use appropriate font sizes
  • Maintain proper line height
  • Create clear hierarchy

Conclusion

Mastering typography takes practice, but the results are worth it. Well-designed typography can transform a good website into a great one.
